Request deletion of your account and data

JP's Astro Research users may request deletion of their account and associated service records controlled by the developer, even without installing the app.

  1. In the app, open Google Account → Request account deletion; or
  2. Email jayaprakash3195@gmail.com from your registered Gmail account with the subject JP's Astro Research Account Deletion Request.

Include your registered email address. Do not send passwords, OTPs or payment-card details. After verifying the request, account-linked data under our control will be processed for deletion, except records required for legal or payment purposes. Data stored only on your phone and optional backup copies in your own Google Drive must be removed separately.
